Accessibility Compliance Statement & Support

EnterpriseCore is fully designed, audited, and tested against World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA & AAA.

Assistive Technology Verification:

  • Screen readers: NVDA, JAWS, and Apple VoiceOver fully supported.
  • Keyboard only: 100% operable without mouse interaction; zero keyboard traps.
  • High contrast ratio: Minimum 7:1 for body copy and 4.5:1 for interactive states.
  • Semantic landmarks: Standardized landmark regions allow rapid landmark navigation.

If you experience any accessibility barrier or have inquiries, please contact our 24/7 accessibility team at accessibility@enterprise.corp.
